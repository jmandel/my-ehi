MAR_ADDL_INFO
Description:
Table for contact level additional information such as barcode scans.

Primary Key
Column NameOrdinal Position
ORDER_ID1
CONTACT_DATE_REAL2
LINE3

Column Information
NameTypeDiscontinued?
1ORDER_IDNUMERICNo
The unique identifier (.1 item) for the order record.
2CONTACT_DATE_REALFLOATNo
A unique contact date in decimal format. The integer portion of the number indicates the date of contact. The digits after the decimal distinguish different contacts on the same date and are unique for each contact on that date. For example, .00 is the first/only contact, .01 is the second contact, etc.
3LINEINTEGERNo
The line number for the information associated with this contact. Multiple pieces of information can be associated with this contact.
4SCANNED_BARCODEVARCHARNo
The raw data captured during barcode scanning.
5NDC_CSN_IDVARCHARNo
The CSN of the corresponding NDC. This can come from either scanning an NDC or entering one in the additional information grid for an administration.
6ORD_IDNUMERICNo
When an ORD ID barcode is scanned, this is the order ID.
7SCANNED_DOSEVARCHARNo
The scanned dose from the barcode if it is available.
8SCANNED_DOSE_UNIT_C_NAMEVARCHARNo
The scanned dose unit category number from the barcode for the administration, if it is available.
9LOT_NUMVARCHARNo
The associated lot number with this medication administration. This can come from either barcode scanning or entering the lot number in the additional information grid.
10EXP_DATEDATETIMENo
The associated expiration date with this medication administration. This can come from either barcode scanning or entering the expiration date in the additional information grid.
11MED_BILL_PROC_ID_PROC_NAMEVARCHARNo
The name of each procedure.
12SCANNED_CONCNTRTNVARCHARNo
The concentration from a barcode scan or entered as part of additional info.
13MED_BILL_UCL_IDVARCHARNo
The unique ID of the charge dropped for this NDC.
14SCANNED_CONC_UNIT_C_NAMEVARCHARNo
The concentration unit from a barcode scan or entered as part of additional info.
15PSP_IDENTVARCHARNo
The identifier associated with the Patient-Specific Package barcode scan.
