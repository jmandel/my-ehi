CYCLE_CRYO_EMBRYO
Description:
Embryos that were cryopreserved during a specific fertility treatment cycle.

Primary Key
Column NameOrdinal Position
RECORD_ID1
LINE2

Column Information
NameTypeDiscontinued?
1RECORD_IDNUMERICNo
The unique identifier for the cycle record.
2LINEINTEGERNo
The line number for the information associated with this record. Multiple pieces of information can be associated with this record.
3CRYO_EMBRYO_ID_NAMEVARCHARNo
A custom ID title for the cryopreservation. If not set, users will see a generated ID based on the line number, such as "Embryo N."
4CRYO_EMBRYO_DATEDATETIMENo
The date that the embryos were cryopreserved.
5CRYO_EMBRYO_DAYINTEGERNo
The number of days that an embryo has been in culture when it is cryopreserved.
6CRYO_EMBRYO_STAGE_C_NAMEVARCHARNo
The embryo's stage when it's cryopreserved.
7CRYO_EMBRYO_GRADE_C_NAMEVARCHARNo
The embryo's grade at cryopreservation.
8CRYO_EMBRYO_FERT_METHOD_C_NAMEVARCHARNo
The fertilization method (e.g. conventional IVF or ICSI) used for the embryo.
9CRYO_EMBRYO_PGT_A_C_NAMEVARCHARNo
The cryopreserved embryo's PGT-A result.
10CRYO_EMBRYO_PGT_M_C_NAMEVARCHARNo
The cryopreserved embryo's PGT-M result.
11CRYO_EMBRYO_PGT_SR_C_NAMEVARCHARNo
The cryopreserved embryo's PGT-SR result.
12CRYO_EMBRYO_AH_YNVARCHARNo
Whether assisted hatching was performed on the cryopreserved embryo.
13EGG_EMBRYO_GAMETE_SOURCE_C_NAMEVARCHARNo
The cryopreserved embryo's egg source, such as if it was autologous or came from a partner or donor.
14SPERM_EMBRYO_GAMETE_SOURCE_C_NAMEVARCHARNo
The cryopreserved embryo's sperm source, such as a partner or donor.
15EGG_EMBRYO_GAMETE_STATE_C_NAMEVARCHARNo
The cryopreserved embryo's egg state (fresh or thawed) at the beginning of the treatment cycle.
16SPERM_EMBRYO_GAMETE_STATE_C_NAMEVARCHARNo
The cryopreserved embryo's sperm state (fresh or thawed) at the start of the cycle.
17CRYO_EMBRYO_EGG_DONORVARCHARNo
The cryopreserved embryo's egg donor ID.
18CRYO_EMBRYO_SPERM_DONORVARCHARNo
The cryopreserved embryo's semen donor ID.
19CRYO_EMBRYO_COMMENTSVARCHARNo
Comment for an embryo being cryopreserved.
