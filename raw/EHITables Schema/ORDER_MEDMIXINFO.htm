ORDER_MEDMIXINFO
Description:
This table is used to extract ingredient medication information for mixture orders.

Primary Key
Column NameOrdinal Position
ORDER_MED_ID1
LINE2

Column Information
NameTypeDiscontinued?
1ORDER_MED_IDNUMERICNo
The unique ID of the medication order (prescription) record.
2LINEINTEGERNo
The line number for the information associated with this record. Multiple pieces of information can be associated with this record. Each line represents an ingredient of the mixture.
3MEDICATION_ID_MEDICATION_NAMEVARCHARNo
The name of this medication record.
4INGREDIENT_TYPE_C_NAMEVARCHARNo
The ingredient type category ID for an ingredient of a medication mixture, indicating what the type of ingredient such as medication or base.
5MIN_DOSE_AMOUNTNUMERICNo
The minimum ordered dose amount of the ingredient.
6MAX_DOSE_AMOUNTNUMERICNo
The maximum ordered dose amount of the ingredient.
7DOSE_UNIT_C_NAMEVARCHARNo
The med & dose unit category ID for an ingredient of a medication mixture.
8FREQUENCY_IDVARCHARNo
The unique identifier of the frequency record associated with the mixture ingredient.
9FREQUENCY_ID_FREQ_NAMEVARCHARNo
The name of the frequency record.
10MIN_INFUSION_RATENUMERICNo
The minimum infusion rate amount of the ingredient.
11MAX_INFUSION_RATENUMERICNo
The maximum infusion rate amount of the ingredient.
12RATE_UNIT_C_NAMEVARCHARNo
The infusion rate unit for the ingredient.
13SEPARATE_BAG_YNVARCHARNo
Indicates that the ingredient is in a separated bag. 'Y' indicates that the ingredient is in a separated bag. 'N' or NULL indicates that the ingredient is not in a separated bag.
14NONFORMULARY_YNVARCHARNo
Indicates whether the ingredient is not on a hospital formulary. 'Y' indicates that the ingredient is not on a hospital formulary. 'N' or NULL indicates that the ingredient is on a hospital formulary.
15SELECTIONVARCHARNo
Indicates the selection status of the ingredient.
16MIN_CALC_DOSE_AMTNUMERICNo
Indicates the minimum calculated dose amount for the ingredient.
17MAX_CALC_DOSE_AMTNUMERICNo
Indicates the maximum calculated dose amount for the ingredient.
18CALC_DOSE_UNIT_C_NAMEVARCHARNo
The med & dose unit category ID for the calculated dose of the medication order.
19DOSE_CALC_INFOVARCHARNo
Indicates the calculated steps to get the calculated minimum or maximum calculated dose.
20CALCDOSAMT_PDAYNUMERICNo
Calculated dose amount per day.
21RXM_CALC_AMTUNTPD_C_NAMEVARCHARNo
Unit for calculated dose amount per day.
22RXM_ADDS_VOLUME_YNVARCHARNo
Indicates whether the ingredient adds volume to the mixture. 'Y' indicates that the ingredient does contribute volume to the mixture. 'N' or NULL indicates that the ingredient does not contribute volume to the mixture.
23DOSE_CALC_WARNINGVARCHARNo
A warning about whether a medication mixture's final dose was rounded by more than a specified percentage.
24RXM_ADDS_WT_YNVARCHARNo
Indicates whether the ingredient adds weight to the mixture. 'Y' indicates that the ingredient does contribute weight to the mixture. 'N' or NULL indicates that the ingredient does not contribute weight to the mixture.
25RXM_DISP_QTYNUMERICNo
Stores the ordered dispense quantity.
26RXM_DISP_UNIT_C_NAMEVARCHARNo
Stores the ordered ingredient dispense quantity unit.
27RXM_RATIONUMERICNo
Stores ingredient ratio.
28RXM_RATIO_UNIT_C_NAMEVARCHARNo
Stores the ingredient ratio unit.
29RXM_DOSE_RND_ACK_C_NAMEVARCHARNo
The category number for the acknowledgement reason given by the user to override a dose rounding warning on this component.
30COMP_RX_NUM_RAWVARCHARNo
The unformatted prescription numbers for each component in a medication that should be administered with multiple tablets of different strengths.
31COMP_RX_NUM_FMTVARCHARNo
The formatted prescription numbers for each component in a medication that should be administered with multiple tablets of different strengths.
32COMP_SIGVARCHARNo
The medication instructions for a single component of a mixture comprised of multiple medications with different tablet strengths.
33COMP_DISPENSE_TEXTVARCHARNo
This column stores the dispense amount added by a user.
