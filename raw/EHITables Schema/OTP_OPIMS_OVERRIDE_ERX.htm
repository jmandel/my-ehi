OTP_OPIMS_OVERRIDE_ERX
Description:
This group of tables stores information about the override that a clinician entered when they chose to not use the products chosen by outpatient Intelligent Medication Selection (IMS). This table stores the list of Medications (ERX) chosen by outpatient IMS, found in the OPIMS Overridden Suggested Product (I OTP 11603) item.
